- Saas application
- Probably Ruby on Rails API with React Frontend
- Desktop and mobile support
- Acts as a communications portal between parents, teachers and possibly students
- Parents and students can have accounts
- Maintain relationships between parents and students
- A parent can have multiple students, a student can have multiple parents
- Login through username and password or gmail. Maybe eventually other login providers
- A student has a school that they attend. This can chnage over time. We need to maintain a history.
- A parent can have students at multiple schools. They should also be able to see the relevant history from previous schools.
- A school administrator has an account they can log in with and manage their school
- There will probably be more than one administrator - might include principals, but also office staff
- Open question: If a school administrator has kids at a school, do they use a separate login to see their kids stuff, or is it just a different view from their one login?
- At the start of a school year (or any time later), new students get enrolled by an administrator
  - Their parents would need to be notified that they needed to create an account and there needs to be a way to link their kids to their account
  - Some options 
    - the system generates a one-time-use code for each student enrollment and then that gets printed out and sent home with the kids
    - Kids provide their parent's email addresses, when are entered into the system. Parents then get an email with a link they can click on which ties their account together with their kids. Might be problematic - a kid could provide their own email and pretend to be their parent? Maybe not a problem?
- A school board may buy a license for all schools in the board - Probably a board level adminstrator function to assign the head administrator for each school?
- Different roles? School Head Administrator (the principal), School Assistant Administrator (the admin staff), Board Administrator, Teacher
